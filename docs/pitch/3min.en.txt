[0:00-0:22] THE PROBLEM

>>> NOT SPOKEN: Cloudbite open on the project screen. Do not click yet. <<<

Every product team hits the same question: should we launch this?
You need product, finance, security, engineering and design views.
Ask one chatbot to play all five, and you get five confident paragraphs
you can't check. Where did that number come from? What was assumed?


[0:22-0:42] WHAT CLOUDBITE IS

Cloudbite gives one shared project to a team of expert AI personas.
Each expert gets its own Daytona workspace, an isolated computer to run Python and write files,
with reasoning from Nosana inference through our server.
Each one returns artifacts, evidence, assumptions and a structured opinion.


[0:42-1:56] DEMO

Our example project: should we launch a hundred-expert AI advisory service?
This is the shared brief.

>>> NOT SPOKEN: open the expert list. Pause 2 s. <<<

>>> NOT SPOKEN: select the finance expert, open the cost-model artifact. Pause 3 s. <<<

Here's the point. Finance didn't just say "it's expensive."

Prices stay variables, because nobody has measured them, and the file says so.

>>> NOT SPOKEN: go to the decision view / opinion distribution. Pause 2 s. <<<
