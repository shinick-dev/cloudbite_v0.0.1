3. CLAIM BOUNDARIES - say these, never stretch them

OK to say
- Five roles selected from a twelve-role catalogue; up to three run at once.
  Five experts and three concurrent are the designed limits.
- A hundred experts is the direction / vision, not current capacity.
- The pitch walkthrough is scripted and labeled as such.
- The real console connects to the live API, supports explicit browser demo
  mode, cancellation and real artifact retrieval.
- Local checks pass: npm run check, 308 persona checks with registry
  cross-check, 18 runtime checks including a real local Python runner through
  validation, with fake (stand-in) Daytona and Nosana. This is local
  integration proof, not paid-provider success.
- A recorded run (demo/evidence.json) created three real Daytona sandboxes,
  executed Python remotely in each, and cleaned them up.
- Confidence is each model's self-reported estimate.
- Next step: first full run with live Nosana inference.

Do NOT say or imply
- That live Nosana inference has already run, or that any provider
  end-to-end flow was verified in the new console.
- That the recorded Daytona run produced model advice: its stance and
  confidence were deterministic seeded placeholders, not Nosana, not a model.
- That the recorded run shows parallel execution (the script ran sandboxes
  one after another) or that its timings are benchmarks (one-off run).
- That confidence is calibrated accuracy or agreement of real experts.
- Any customer demand, quotes, pricing, revenue or ARR. Target customer
  (early product teams making launch decisions) is a hypothesis.
- That a hundred sandboxes have been run.

4. LIKELY QUESTIONS - short honest answers

Q: Did Nosana actually run?
A: The gateway path is implemented for a configured endpoint; the first live
   end-to-end run is our next verification. Today's proof is local, with
   stand-ins, plus real Daytona sandboxes in a separate recorded run.
Q: Is the confidence meaningful?
A: It is self-reported and uncalibrated. We show it as a signal next to the
   evidence, not as a probability of being right.
Q: Why not just ask one big model?
A: You get an answer you can't inspect. Here each opinion comes with the
   file, numbers and assumptions behind it.
Q: Business model?
A: Not decided yet. Our hypothesis is the customer, early product teams
   making launch decisions; we have not validated pricing or demand.
