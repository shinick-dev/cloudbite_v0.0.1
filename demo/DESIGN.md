# Design System: Cloudbite Pitch Preview & Access Guide

Scope: two self-contained demo surfaces. `demo/cloudbite-pitch.html` is the offline pitch simulation (inline CSS/JS, no external requests). `demo/access-qr.html` and the served `/cloudbite/access` page are both generated by `tools/build-cloudbite-access.mjs`. This file records their actual values. They share the console's navy / blue / white grammar (see root `DESIGN.md`), but their tokens are their own and are not identical to it.

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The finished console, replayed"** (a descriptive label for the pitch's role, not a separately approved metaphor).

The pitch shows the user-pinned frame: a 70px navy header, a 224px light sidebar, a central workspace with a 394px dark stream, and a sticky right rail. The pitch adds a Decision board, a list of selected experts in the sidebar, and a scripted replay (Brief → Workspaces → Evidence → Decision, about 30 seconds at 1× and 10 seconds at 3×). Copy is English. Every number, file and line of activity is authored example data, and the page says so in several places. The access guide is a lighter single page with the same navy header: a QR code on one side, three steps on the other.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Same frame as the console, with pitch-only data: decision board, expert list, replay controls.
- Simulation is always disclosed: header badge, sidebar note, stream badge and footer, page footer.
- 12px is the working text size in panels; one bright blue (`#1f6feb`) on the pitch, Tailwind-style blue (`#2563eb`) on the access guide.
- The QR is a deterministic encoding of the existing request URL, not an AI-generated visual.

## Colors

### Primary
- **Pitch Navy** (`navy`): header, decision-card gradient start (to `#1b3466`), toast. `navy-2` is the header-button hover.
- **Signal Blue** (`blue`, deeper `blue-dark`): primary buttons, the selected nav item, done step marks, progress bar, links, focus ring. The support direction also uses `blue` here (the console uses green).
- **Blue tints** (`blue-50`, `blue-100`): hover washes, the inspect card, aggregation note, avatars, step connectors.
- **Access Navy / Access Blue** (`access-navy`, `access-blue`, hover `#1d4ed8`): the access guide's own header and primary link button.

### Secondary
- **Conditional** (`amber` on `amber-bg`) and **Concern** (`red` on `red-bg`): pills and counts. Distribution segments use lighter fills: `#3b82f6` support, `#d9a441` conditional, `#c4664f` concern, `#d6e1f1` pending.

### Tertiary
- **Simulation Amber** (`sim-amber`): text of the header "Pitch simulation · scripted replay" badge, the stream badge and system rows in the stream.

### Neutral
- `canvas` page, `paper` cards, `sidebar` rail background, `line` borders; `ink` headings, `text` body, `muted` secondary. Stream set (`stream`, `stream-line`, `stream-text`) for the dark log only. `access-bg` is the access page canvas, and `qr-dark` the QR module colour on white.

### Named Rules
**The Say-It's-Scripted Rule.** No surface shows simulated output without a nearby label: "Simulation · not a terminal", "Each line is prepared example activity embedded in this page. No commands run and no provider is contacted.", and a footer stating that reliability is not yet calibrated.

## Typography

**Font:** `"Segoe UI", system-ui, -apple-system, Arial` (the pitch puts Segoe UI first; the access guide uses `'Segoe UI', Arial`). **Mono:** `Consolas, "SFMono-Regular"` for times, file names and the version tag. No web fonts.

### Hierarchy
- **Access display** (700, 36px/1.25; 29px ≤800px): the access guide H1, "Try it in your own sandbox."
- **Decision** (600, 26px/1.4, max 24ch; 22px ≤760px): the decision-card headline.
- **Scores** (32px / 26px): self-reported confidence figures.
- **Wordmark** (700, 23px): lower-case "cloudbite" plus a mono `v0.0.1`.
- **Headline** (19px/1.35): the project question.
- **Body** (14px/1.6): the base size. Most panel copy, stream rows, steps and labels are set at 12px.

## Layout

- **Pitch shell:** a sticky 70px header, then a `224px | 1fr` grid. The sidebar is sticky with its own scroll. Content is a `1fr | 336px` grid (main | rail) with a 20px gap and 20px 24px padding. The rail is sticky (`top: 90px`) and scrolls independently.
- **Main:** project card → view tabs (Expert workspaces / Decision board) → workspace panel with a stream-scope segmented control, Clear view and Full screen → 394px stream → five file cards.
- **Breakpoints:** ≤1279px file cards go to 3 columns. ≤1199px the rail turns into a static two-column grid below main, with the inspector spanning on the right. ≤860px the sidebar becomes a top row and selected experts scroll horizontally as 146px cards. ≤760px the header is 62px and not sticky, the badge shortens to "Simulation", the provider note and stage steps move inline, the stream is 380px with a 2-column row grid, file cards use 2 columns, and the footer stacks.
- **Access guide:** max width 1120px, a `420px | 1fr` grid (QR panel | steps) that stacks at ≤800px. A print stylesheet hides the actions.

## Elevation & Depth

Mostly flat: white cards with 1px `line` borders on `canvas`. Soft shadows appear only for selection (`0 1px 3px` at low alpha on the selected expert, view tab and pressed segment) and for the transient toast. The decision card gets its depth from a navy gradient, not a shadow. Replay jumps pulse a 4px blue ring (`focus-section`, 1s), which is disabled under reduced motion.

## Shapes

Slightly tighter radii than the console: 8px buttons and selects, 9px nav and expert rows, 11px file cards, 12px stream and decision card, 13px cards, 999px pills, circles for avatars and step marks. On the access guide, the QR panel is 14px, the note is 12px and the buttons are 8px with a 44px minimum height.

## Components

### Buttons
Primary is solid blue with white 12px/600 text, 36px minimum height and hover `blue-dark`. Secondary is white with a `line` border and hover `blue-50`. Text buttons are blue on transparent. In the header, secondary buttons are transparent with a `#4a6390` border. Disabled opacity is 0.45. Focus is a 3px blue outline with a 3px offset.

### Navigation
Home / Guide / Help / Settings with 17px stroked SVG icons. The current item is solid blue with white text (`aria-current="page"`), and hover is `blue-50`. Below the nav, the sidebar lists the 5 selected experts (avatar, role, state), the Daytona and Nosana illustrative rows, a disclosure note and a "Try a sandbox" link to `access-qr.html`, labelled as the real request flow and not part of the replay.

### Execution stream (signature)
A dark panel with the title "Execution stream", the subtitle "Scripted replay of the example run · read-only" and an amber "Simulation · not a terminal" badge. Rows use a `46px 132px 1fr` grid (time, actor, message). It has no input. **Clear view** hides rows from this view only ("The run data is unchanged") and becomes **Restore view**. **Full screen** sets a fixed overlay and locks body scroll. Escape exits and returns focus to the button.

### Decision board and rail
The decision card shows the overall direction or "Interim tally · conclusion on hold", the count of valid opinions, the incomplete count and "Reliability status awaiting external validation". Counts and the distribution bar are computed from completed opinions only. An aggregation note states the unweighted rule. The rail holds Step progress (4 stages), What to inspect, a tip ("Final confidence is self-reported, not accuracy") and the selected-expert inspector with Opinion / Persona / Work order / Artifacts / Activity tabs.

### Access guide
The page has a navy header, an H1 and lead copy, and a white QR panel. The panel holds the QR (SVG; PNG and SVG downloads as data URIs), the request URL as a link, "Open request form" (primary) and "Save QR image". Next to it are three numbered steps. Step 1 quotes the Korean form option `혼자 복습할 개인 실습실` and its English gloss. The page also has a pale-blue note ("The QR itself grants no access") and a footer link to the owner inbox, which is access-gated.

### Keyboard
Tablists support ArrowLeft/ArrowRight (wrapping), Home and End. A skip link leads to main content. The replay timer is `aria-hidden` and sits outside the status region.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** keep the pinned console frame (70px navy header, 224px sidebar, 394px dark stream) when updating the pitch.
- **Do** keep every simulated element labelled, and keep the page free of external `http(s)` requests.
- **Do** regenerate both access outputs from `tools/build-cloudbite-access.mjs` and keep the QR pointing to the existing request URL.

### Don't:
- **Don't** assume pitch tokens equal console tokens. Navy, canvas, line and radii differ slightly, and support is blue here but green in the console.
- **Don't** present the pitch's numbers, files or provider rows as real execution, calibrated accuracy or demand evidence.
- **Don't** add a command prompt to the stream or style the QR as a generated illustration.
